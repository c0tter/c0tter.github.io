Chair’s Address — 2025/26 in Review
Celebration SC 10 mins
▾
Stephen took over the chair from Jon Radcliffe in 2025. This is a look back at what the club achieved over two years and a chance to thank the people who made it happen.

What we set out to do

  • Keep Bonfire Night and the Santa Rounds going strong as the community events Poynton relies on
  • Give away more money to better causes, more transparently
  • Give the club proper structure — constitution, handbook, clear roles — to make it easier for the next generation of members to pick up and run with
  • Grow the membership and bring in fresh energy

What we delivered

🍽
Bonfire Night — continued and improved
Two successful Bonfire Nights. 2025 saw 19 traffic volunteers (Cricket Club 8, Richmond Rovers 5, Lacrosse 6) keeping the roads flowing, and 90 cleanup volunteers across 10 groups clearing the site the morning after. The 2025 debrief produced the most detailed action plan we’ve ever had — lighting, ticketing, gate management, site layout, bar, and fireworks all being improved for 2026. Jon Fuller has taken over as Bonfire Officer with James Price stepping up as Deputy.
🎅
Santa Rounds — growing year on year
Santa 2025/26: £1,562 raised, £815 profit — more than double the previous year’s £339 profit. Matt Anthony ran a strong operation. The sleigh has been serviced and is in better shape than it’s been in years.
💰
£76,200 raised over two years
£42,500 in 2024/25 and £33,700 in 2025/26. Every penny from Bonfire Night and the Santa Rounds. Allocated to local schools, community groups, and charitable causes across Poynton and the surrounding area.
📝
Constitution & Handbook — built from scratch
Dan Robinson drafted a full constitution giving the club legal and structural clarity it never had before. A member handbook was also created to help new members understand roles and how we operate. Both are being adopted at this AGM.
🤝
New roles & succession planning
This year saw the creation of a Mental Health Officer (Dr Chris McHague), a Deputy Treasurer (Jim Hirst shadowing Tom), and a formal Vice Chair (Matt Anthony). The succession pipeline from DBO → BO → Vice Chair → Chair is now mapped out years ahead.
👥
Membership growing
10 active members, 7 honorary members, 5 potential new members in the pipeline. Honorary membership formalised and growing as past members stay connected.

Thank yous

  • Dan Robinson — constitution, handbook, and marketing. An enormous amount of work, done brilliantly.
  • Tom Williamson — treasurer and IT officer for a second year. Keeps the finances clean and the tools running.
  • Jon Fuller — stepping up as Bonfire Officer and bringing serious organisation to the biggest event of our year.
  • John Dunlop — Community Officer and honorary member. Institutional knowledge and steady hands.
  • Matt Anthony — ran a superb Santa operation and is ready to take on the chairmanship.
  • Jon Radcliffe — for handing over the chair in good shape and continuing to contribute as Membership Officer.
  • James Price, Matt Wallace, Jim Hirst, Dr Chris McHague — for stepping into new roles and building the future of the club.
  • Pete Cole — for years of service as Programme Officer. A tough act to follow.
  • And to every member who turned up on a cold November night, pulled on a Santa hat, or gave a Saturday to the lockup. This is you.

Adoption of the New Constitution
Vote Required Dan R 15 mins
▾
Dan Robinson drafted PDRT’s first proper constitution this year, pulling together everything from RTBI guidance, decisions made at council level, and the club’s own history. It’s been reviewed by the council and has buy-in from all active members. Tonight we adopt it formally.

What the constitution covers

  • Membership tiers: Active, Honorary, and Potential — with clear criteria for each
  • Officer roles: Defined responsibilities and term lengths for every position
  • Succession pipeline: DBO → BO → Vice Chair → Chair mapped out explicitly
  • Voting rules: Quorum, majority requirements, and what decisions need a formal vote
  • Eligibility for Vice Chair: Completed at least one full year as a Council Officer, OR demonstrated active participation in Bonfire operations, OR supported by a majority vote at AGM
  • Charitable governance: Trustee responsibilities, Charity Commission obligations
  • Financial controls: Spending authority, donations process, accounting year

Journey to this point

  • Drafted by Dan Robinson, incorporating RTBI guidance and council discussions
  • Reviewed by JD, Jon Fuller, Matt Anthony, Matt Wallace, and Tom Williamson
  • Small amendment: Vice Chair eligibility changed from “and” to “or” to allow flexibility
  • Final version shared with all council members in March 2026
Why this matters Without a formal constitution, every handover depends on institutional memory. With one, any new member can pick up the handbook and understand how PDRT works. This is the foundation for the next 50 years.
