6
Short Financial Year
Vote Required TW 15 mins
▾
In simple terms: Our accounting year currently runs May–April. That means our two biggest money-making events (Bonfire in November, Santa in December) fall across two financial years, making it hard to see the full picture when planning donations. We want to fix that.

What we're proposing

  • Run a short accounting period: May–December 2025 to bridge the gap
  • Then switch to calendar year reporting (Jan–Dec) going forward

Why it's better

  • After bonfire and Santa, we'll know exactly how much we raised that year
  • Donations for the coming year can be planned with the full picture
  • The AGM reports on fresh, relevant numbers instead of year-old figures
  • Frees up April for trustee updates and bank admin
Charity commission As a registered charity we have to keep proper accounts and submit them annually. Tom confirms the accounts are up to date and this change is straightforward to implement.
Resolution #1
To approve a short accounting period (May–December 2025) and switch to calendar year reporting from January 2026 onwards.
