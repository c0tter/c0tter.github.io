Short Financial Year
Vote Required TW 15 mins
▾
The problem: The current accounting year runs May–April. This means the AGM (held in April/May) reports on figures that feel like last year's news. Bonfire (November) and Santa (December) income falls across two accounting periods, making donation planning unclear.

Proposed change

  • Run a short accounting period: May–December 2025
  • Switch to calendar year reporting (Jan–Dec) going forward

Benefits

  • After bonfire and Santa, the full year's income is known
  • Donations for the coming year can be planned with clarity
  • AGM reports on fresh, relevant figures
  • Frees up April for trustee updates and bank mandate work
Trust deed compliance Trustees must comply with the Charities Act 1993 regarding: keeping accounting records, preparing annual statements, auditing/independent examination, and transmitting accounts to the Charity Commission. TW confirms accounts are up to date and ready.
