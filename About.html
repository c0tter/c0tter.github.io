About Poynton Round Table

Poynton Round Table is a friendly, social group for people aged 18–45 who want to meet new people, try new activities and support the local community. We organise a wide range of events — from pub nights and meals out to adventure activities like kayaking, indoor skydiving and go-karting.

We meet regularly (typically twice a month) and welcome visitors to attend a few events for free. If you'd like to find out more or come along to an event, get in touch via our Contact page.
