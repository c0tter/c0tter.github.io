Poynton Round Table Film Club

We have a small film subgroup for members who enjoy cinema and film nights. The group organises trips to local screenings, festival visits (for example the Banff Mountain Film Festival in Stockport) and informal film nights at members' homes. The Film Club covers a wide range of tastes — from blockbusters to independent and documentary films.

If you’re interested in joining the Film Club, let us know via the contact page and we’ll add you to the mailing list or WhatsApp group.
