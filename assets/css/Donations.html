Request funding / Make a donation

We focus our donations on local causes. Over the past 10 years we have given more than £150,000 to charities, clubs and community projects in and around Poynton. These funds come from local support of our fundraising activities such as the Poynton Bonfire and the Santa Rounds.

If you represent a local charity, community group or good cause and have a funding need, we'd love to hear from you. Please send a short outline of your organisation, the project you need funding for, and contact details to the address on our contact page.
