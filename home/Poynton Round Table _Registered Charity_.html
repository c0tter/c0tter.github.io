What we do

We organise social activities — from nights out and pub socials to outdoor adventures and community projects. All activities help build friendships and raise money for local charities.

Try new activities

Recent activities include kayaking, rock climbing, go-karting, brewery tours and comedy nights.

Meet new people

Joining is a great way to meet people from across Poynton and nearby towns — and to make long-lasting friendships.

Give back locally

We raise funds each year for local charities through our events, such as the Poynton Bonfire and Christmas float.
