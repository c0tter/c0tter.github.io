About Poynton Round Table

Poynton Round Table is a friendly social group for people aged 18–45 who want to meet new people, try new activities and support the local community. We organise a wide range of events — from pub nights and meals out to adventure activities like kayaking, indoor skydiving and go-karting.

Why join?

  • It's great fun: Social and adventurous activities across the year.
  • Meet new people: Make friends from across Poynton and the surrounding areas.
  • Give back: We raise money for local charities every year.

How it works

We typically meet twice a month. Guests are welcome to join a few events for free before deciding to become members. Membership is open to men aged 18–45 and involves a small monthly subscription used to fund social events.

Want to join?

Visit our Contact page to get in touch or come along to an event — we’d love to meet you.
