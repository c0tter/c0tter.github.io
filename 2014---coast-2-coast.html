Coast 2 Coast - August 2014

So we must be mad, but in January 2014 when people are setting New Year’s Resolutions some bright spark suggested a Coast 2 Coast Cycle Challenge this year….  So on the 29th of August 2014, 13 of us headed up north to ride the 186 miles from Whitehaven to Scarborough over 3 days.  Madness, but an amazing experience.

The Route and Elevation

186 miles - Whitehaven to Scarborough with 2 stops in 3 days.
Below are details of the 3 days of cycling, including route, distance and elevation.  There seems to be lots of up hills, and some very long and steep!

Day 1 - Whitehaven to Penrith - Friday 29th August 2014

Day 2 - Penrith to Middlesbrough - Saturday 30th August 2014
