What we do

Poynton Round Table is a friendly, social group for people aged 18-45 who want to meet new people, try new activities and support the local community. We meet regularly and welcome visitors to attend a few events for free.

🏊

Try new activities

Kayaking, rock climbing, go-karting, brewery tours, comedy nights, indoor skydiving and much more.

🤝

Meet new people

Join people from across Poynton and nearby towns to make long-lasting friendships over shared experiences.

❤️

Give back locally

We raise funds each year for local charities through events like the Poynton Bonfire and Christmas float.

Donations & Impact

Over the past 10 years we have given more than £150,000 to charities, clubs and community projects in and around Poynton.

£150k+ donated over 10 years
£20k+ donated in 2022 alone
40+ local groups supported

Recent recipients include

Poynton Pedals for All Poynton Community Larder Poynton Heritage Garden RT Children's Wish Poynton Community Support Poynton 5th Guides Self Care Matters Richmond Rovers Girls Bramhall Scouts Poynton Sports Club Buxton Mountain Rescue Edale Mountain Rescue Local school PTAs
