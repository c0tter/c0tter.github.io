About Poynton Round Table

We’re a volunteer group in Poynton focused on fun, community and fundraising. Our events raise money for local projects, and we award small grants to groups in need.

Members range from people interested in outdoor activities to those wanting to support local charities — all are welcome to contact us and try a meeting.
